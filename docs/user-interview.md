# User interview: one real artist

**Who:** one digital artist or illustrator who finds holding a stylus painful or impossible for long
sessions. Ask permission to quote them by first name (or anonymously) in the README, the demo and the video.
Record the answer word for word (with consent, a phone voice memo is fine). Never paraphrase into a quote.

**Rules:** do not ask about or record a diagnosis; ask what the hand does and what gets in the way. Ten
minutes is enough. Show them Steady only after question 3, so the first answers aren't about our design.

## Questions

1. **How do you draw today, and what does a long session cost your hand?** (Follow up: what makes you stop?)
2. **What have you tried to keep drawing — smoothing settings, a different grip, a tablet, voice, other
   tools — and what did each get wrong?**
3. **When would you use voice to change a brush or colour, and when would you never talk to your computer?**
   (Shared studio, class, home, late at night.)
4. *(After a 2-minute try of Steady with a resting hand.)* **What felt different from holding a stylus, and
   what was the first thing that annoyed you?**
5. **If you could change one thing so you would use this on a bad day, what would it be?**
